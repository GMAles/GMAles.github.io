## bio

Psychiatrist and epidemiologist trained at Universidad Autónoma de Madrid and Hospital Universitario La Paz, then Columbia and Harvard's CAUSALab. Now at Mount Sinai, working on causal inference for psychiatric epidemiology — suicide risk, first-episode psychosis, and the social determinants of severe mental illness. Believer in public psychiatry and Medicare for All: better evidence should mean better, more universal care.
